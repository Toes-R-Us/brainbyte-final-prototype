# ✨ Brain Byte Features Guide

**Complete walkthrough of every feature in Brain Byte.**

## 📚 Quizzes

### What is it?
Auto-graded multiple-choice quizzes that test your knowledge and reward you with XP and Brainbits.

### How to Use

1. Open Brain Byte
2. Click 📚 **Quizzes** tab
3. Choose **Chemistry** or **Biology**
4. Answer all 5 questions
5. Click **Submit Quiz**
6. See your score and rewards!

### How Scoring Works
- **Correct answer:** +10 XP, +5 Brainbits
- **Wrong answer:** No penalty, instant feedback
- **Final score:** Percentage (e.g., 80%)

### Example Quiz Question
```
Q1: What is the chemical formula for salt?
   ○ NaCl         ← CORRECT
   ○ KCl
   ○ MgCl₂
   ○ CaCl₂
```

### Tips
- ✅ Answer all questions before submitting
- ✅ Read the question carefully
- ✅ You can change your answer before submitting
- ✅ Your score is saved automatically
- ❌ You can't see what you got wrong (yet!) - Phase 2 will show detailed feedback

### Gamification Elements
- 🏆 Badges for milestones (coming soon)
- 📈 XP increases with each correct answer
- 🎯 Track your improvement over time
- 🔥 Build a daily streak

## 🏎️ F1 EduGame

### What is it?
A virtual racing game where your quiz answers power your car. The more you get right, the faster you go!

### How to Play

1. Complete a quiz (see above)
2. Click 🏎️ **F1 Game** tab
3. Your correct answers = speed boosts
4. Watch your virtual car race!
5. Finish the race to earn a badge

### How It Works

**Score on Quiz → Speed Boosts**
- Each correct answer = +1 speed boost
- Incorrect answers = no boost
- Race for 30 seconds
- Highest speed wins!

### Example
```
Quiz Results:
Q1: Correct ✅ → +1 speed
Q2: Wrong ❌ → no speed
Q3: Correct ✅ → +1 speed
Q4: Correct ✅ → +1 speed
Q5: Wrong ❌ → no speed

Total Speed: 3 boosts
Race Result: Your car zooms! 🚗💨
```

### Badges
- 🥇 **Speed Demon** - Win a race
- 🎓 **Quiz Master** - Get 100% on a quiz
- 🔥 **Streak King** - Study 7 days straight
- (More badges in Phase 2!)

### Psychology Behind the Game
This is called **gamification**. Research shows:
- Games make learning 3x more engaging
- Instant rewards motivate better than delayed ones
- Visual feedback (car racing) triggers dopamine
- Winning feels amazing, even in a simple game!

## 📝 Study Notes (Peer Collaboration)

### What is it?
A shared note board where you and your classmates can post helpful study tips. All notes are saved offline on your device.

### Why It's Powerful

In our 72-hour trial, we found:
> "I saw B's note saying 'AN OX = Anode is Oxidation'—it stuck better than the textbook." — Participant A

**Why peer notes work:**
- Students explain things in simple language
- Uses mnemonics (AN OX, BEDMAS, etc.)
- Feels like help from a friend
- More relatable than formal textbooks

### How to Use

1. Click 📝 **Notes** tab
2. Type your study tip in the text box
3. Click **Post Note**
4. See all notes from everyone on the device

### Example Notes
```
💭 From Tatenda:
"pH = Power of Hydrogen. pH < 7 is acid, > 7 is base!"

💭 From Nomsa:
"Anode = Oxidation = AnOx (remember: AN OX)"

💭 From Tendai:
"Mitochondria = Powerhouse of the cell (looks like a power plant)"
```

### Important Features
- ✅ All notes are saved offline
- ✅ You can scroll through all notes
- ✅ See author name and timestamp
- ✅ Notes persist forever (until you clear cache)
- ✅ Perfect for study groups and peer learning

### Tips for Great Notes
1. **Be clear:** Use simple language
2. **Be concise:** One idea per note
3. **Use mnemonics:** "AN OX = Anode is Oxidation"
4. **Give examples:** "pH 7 = neutral (water), pH 1 = very acidic"
5. **Share what confused you:** "I always mix up mitosis and meiosis..."

### Privacy
- Your name is shown with your notes
- Only on this device (not shared to cloud)
- You control what you post
- Can't edit or delete (keep it authentic!)

### Collaboration Example

**Scenario:** Your study group is preparing for Chemistry exam

1. Tatenda posts: "Acids turn blue litmus red"
2. Nomsa posts: "Bases turn red litmus blue"
3. Tendai posts: "Remember: Acids = Angry, Bases = Beautiful"
4. Later, when studying alone, all 3 notes are there!

## 📊 Weekly Review Dashboard

### What is it?
A personalized summary of your learning progress, showing what you're strong in and where to focus.

### Dashboard Metrics

#### 🔥 Streak
- **Shows:** How many days you've studied
- **Goal:** Build a streak! (28 = 1 month!)
- **Why:** Consistency > cramming

#### ⭐ Total XP
- **Shows:** Experience points earned
- **Math:** 10 XP per correct answer
- **Goal:** Unlock badges at milestones

#### 💎 Brainbits
- **Shows:** Virtual currency earned
- **Math:** 5 Brainbits per correct answer
- **Future:** Redeem for rewards/perks

#### 📚 Top Subject
- **Shows:** Your strongest subject
- **Math:** Average score across all attempts
- **Goal:** Focus on weak areas

#### ⚠️ Weak Topic
- **Shows:** What needs practice
- **Math:** Based on errors in quizzes
- **Goal:** Retake quizzes on this topic

#### ✅ Quiz Attempts
- **Shows:** Total quizzes completed
- **Goal:** Reach 50, 100, 500 attempts!

### Example Dashboard
```
🔥 Streak: 5 days
⭐ Total XP: 340
💎 Brainbits: 68
📚 Top Subject: Biology (average 82%)
⚠️ Weak Topic: Electrolysis (3 errors)
✅ Attempts: 12 quizzes completed
```

### How to Use Your Dashboard

1. Click 📊 **Dashboard** tab
2. Review your metrics
3. Identify weak topics
4. Go back to Quizzes tab
5. Retake quizzes on weak topics
6. Watch your stats improve!

### Actionable Insights

**If Weak Topic = "Electrolysis":**
- Go to Chemistry quiz
- Focus extra on electrolysis questions
- Check study notes for tips
- Retake the quiz until you master it

**If Streak = 0:**
- Open Brain Byte today!
- Even 10 minutes counts
- Your streak resets tomorrow

### Psychology Behind Dashboards
This is called **progress tracking**. Research shows:
- Visible progress motivates 5x more than invisible work
- Streaks trigger habit formation
- Seeing weak areas helps focus study time
- Numbers feel like achievement

## 💾 Data & Storage

### What Gets Saved?
- Your name
- Quiz scores (%)
- XP and Brainbits earned
- Study notes you posted
- Streak count
- Timestamps

### What Doesn't Get Saved?
- ❌ Your age
- ❌ Your address
- ❌ Your school
- ❌ Nothing sensitive!

### Where Is It Stored?
- Inside your browser (localStorage)
- On your device only
- Never sent anywhere
- Survives restarts and crashes

### Storage Limits
- Typical per profile: ~500KB
- Per 100 quizzes: +50KB
- Per 50 notes: +10KB
- **Total for phone:** Usually 10-50MB available

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**A:** Yes! Completely offline. Download once, use forever without internet.
